About Me

I earned my Associate's Degree in Information Technology from Full Sail University and am currently pursuing my Bachelor's Degree in Cybersecurity at the same university. Throughout my education, I have developed a foundation in information technology, network security, risk management, security policies, and cybersecurity frameworks. My coursework has allowed me to apply these concepts through security assessments, vulnerability management, compliance requirements, and security planning.

My professional background includes administrative support, document processing, customer service, and handling confidential information. These positions strengthened my communication, organization, problem-solving, and attention-to-detail skills.

My long-term goal is to become a Cybersecurity Analyst. I continue building my technical knowledge, participating in cybersecurity seminars, and exploring opportunities for professional growth.
